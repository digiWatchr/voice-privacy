digiwatchr voice – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the digiwatchr voice app for Apple Watch and iPhone ("the App"), published by ARP Solutions (OPC) Private Limited ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Summary

The App does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data with us or with third parties. Your recordings stay on your own devices.

Microphone and audio recordings

  • The App asks for microphone permission on your Apple Watch, and records audio only after you tap Record.
  • Recordings are saved as audio files in the App's private storage on your Apple Watch.
  • When you tap Send to iPhone, the recording is sent directly from your Watch to the paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. It is not sent to our servers or anyone else's.
  • Recordings are not transcribed, analysed, or used for advertising.

Sharing

A recording leaves your devices only if you choose to share it with the iPhone's Share button. The recipient or app you choose (for example Messages, Mail or Files) then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

The App contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and it has no user accounts. We do not collect your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or usage data.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings remain on your devices until you delete them. Uninstalling the App from your Apple Watch and iPhone removes the recordings stored by the App on that device.

Recording other people

If you record conversations or calls that include other people, you are responsible for getting their consent where the law requires it.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
